Our Story

Founded in 2018, Brew Haven began with a simple dream: to create a space where coffee lovers could gather, connect, and savor every moment. What started as a small roastery in a garage has grown into a beloved neighborhood institution.

We source our beans directly from sustainable farms across Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala. Every batch is roasted in-house to bring out unique flavor profiles that celebrate the origin of each bean.
